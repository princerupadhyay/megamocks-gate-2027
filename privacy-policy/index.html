Privacy Policy

This is a starting draft written for a small personal blog, not a substitute for legal advice — worth a proper review, especially against India's DPDP Act, before this goes live for real.

What this site collects

MegaMocks does not require an account and does not knowingly collect personal information beyond what's needed to serve the site and understand basic traffic patterns, such as anonymized analytics data.

Cookies and analytics

Standard analytics tools may be used to understand which pages get read, and this site is technically compatible with future advertising placements. Neither is used to build individual profiles for sale to third parties.

Contact form and email

If you email the address on the Contact page, that email address is used only to reply to you, not added to any marketing list.

Changes to this policy

This page may be updated as the site changes. Check back periodically if this matters to you.
