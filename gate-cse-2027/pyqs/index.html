DSA

Data Structures & Algorithms

PYQ pattern so far: graph traversal and complexity-of-algorithm questions show up almost every year, so those get extra rounds.

OS

Operating Systems

Scheduling numericals and deadlock / banker's algorithm questions are the two areas I'm drilling hardest, since they show up almost every attempt.

CN

Computer Networks

Subnetting numericals and congestion-control questions are the ones I keep flagging for a redo.

DBMS

DBMS

Query-output prediction and normalization-form identification are the recurring PYQ types I'm tracking.

COA

Computer Organization & Architecture

Pipeline-hazard numericals are where I'm losing the most time right now, so those get solved slowly and deliberately rather than rushed.

DL

Digital Logic

K-map minimization and flip-flop-based sequential circuit questions are the recurring types I drill.

DM

Discrete Mathematics

Graph theory and counting-based questions are the ones I currently get wrong most often, so they go first in every revision cycle.

TOC

Theory of Computation

Automata-construction questions are manageable so far. Decidability questions are the ones I'm avoiding a little, which usually means I need to face them sooner.

CD

Compiler Design

No PYQ practice yet here — that comes after the first read-through, not before.

EM

Engineering Mathematics

Probability questions are the ones I get wrong most often here — usually from misreading the question, not from not knowing the method.

C

Programming in C

Pointer-arithmetic output-prediction questions are the single type I still get wrong most consistently.
