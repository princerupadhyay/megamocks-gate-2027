Day 45 of my GATE CSE 2027 prep · 28 October 2026

A Surprisingly Easy Start to the Final Compiler Design Chapter

Feeling: relieved, cautiously optimistic, aware of the deadline
Compiler Design Database Management Systems

Wednesday was a relatively smooth day in my GATE CSE 2027 preparation. After dealing with difficult Compiler Design concepts over the previous few days, I finally started the last chapter of the subject: Code Generation and Code Optimization.

And honestly, the first two lectures were surprisingly easy.

I'm not sure whether the chapter is genuinely easy or whether it only feels that way because I'm still at the beginning. But for now, I'm not complaining. After struggling with some of the earlier Compiler Design concepts, a smoother learning experience was welcome.

Starting Code Generation and Code Optimization

The final Compiler Design chapter contains four lectures, with each lecture lasting approximately two and a half to three hours.

On Wednesday, I completed the first two lectures.

Both went quite smoothly, and I found the concepts easy to follow. Compared with some of the topics I had encountered earlier in Compiler Design, this chapter felt much more manageable at the beginning.

However, I don't want to conclude too early that the entire chapter will be easy. Sometimes the initial concepts are straightforward, while the later ones introduce more complicated details.

For now, my experience has been positive, and I'll have a clearer idea after completing the remaining lectures.

The plan is to finish those two lectures on Thursday, 29 October.

The Notes Are Still Pending

Although the lectures went well, there is still unfinished work waiting for me.

I haven't prepared notes for the earlier Compiler Design chapters, including Chapters 3 and 4. So, even after completing the lectures, I still need to consolidate the material.

This is something I need to manage carefully as the October deadline approaches. Completing the lectures is one part of preparation, but the notes and revision are important too.

I don't want to reach the end of October having technically completed the syllabus while leaving a large amount of unfinished work behind.

The remaining days will therefore be important, not just for watching lectures but also for organising what I've already studied.

The Final Three Days of October

My October 31 deadline is now very close.

Thursday, October 29, is reserved for completing the remaining two lectures of Code Generation and Code Optimization. Both are fairly long, so that is already a substantial task for the day.

After that, I'll have Friday and Saturday, October 30 and 31, to prepare the pending Compiler Design notes and finish the entire DBMS subject.

Yes, the target is to complete the whole DBMS subject by October 31, not merely clear a few pending lectures.

It's a tight schedule, and I'll need to manage the available time carefully.

Still, Wednesday's progress gave me a relatively smooth start to the final Compiler Design chapter. I completed two of its four lectures without encountering any major conceptual difficulties.

A Small but Useful Observation

One thing I noticed today is that my perception of a chapter's difficulty can change as I progress through it.

The first two lectures of Code Generation and Code Optimization felt easy. But I haven't completed the chapter yet, so I don't know whether the remaining lectures will feel the same.

Rather than worrying about that in advance, I'll continue with the material and judge the chapter based on the complete experience.

For now, I'm happy that the initial part went smoothly.

Day 45: Halfway Through the Final Chapter

Wednesday wasn't an extraordinary day, but it was a good one for my preparation.

I started Code Generation and Code Optimization, completed two lectures, and found the initial concepts easy to understand. The notes from earlier chapters are still pending, and I have a tight schedule ahead of me.

Thursday is for completing the remaining lectures. Friday and Saturday are for the pending Compiler Design notes and finishing DBMS.

The October deadline is almost here, and the next three days will determine how much of that plan I can actually complete.

Day 45 complete. Two lectures down, two to go, and the October 31 target still stands.
