Computer Networks

First pass done — notes complete

Networks felt abstract until I started drawing the layers out myself instead of just reading the OSI model as a list. It's also the subject with the most "wait, which layer does this again" confusion when I started.

First pass through the syllabus is done. Next up is a dedicated week of subnetting and routing numericals, since that's where most of my mock test losses in this subject come from.

Topics I'm covering

  • IP Addressing & Subnetting
  • Routing Algorithms
  • Congestion & Flow Control
  • Application Layer Protocols — DNS, HTTP, FTP
  • Error Detection & Correction
  • Switching — Circuit vs Packet
