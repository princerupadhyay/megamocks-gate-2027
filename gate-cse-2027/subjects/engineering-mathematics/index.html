Engineering Mathematics

Ongoing — weekly problem sets

Engineering Maths was actually where I restarted on day one, since it felt like the least intimidating way back into studying after a long break. It's stayed a steady, low-drama subject since then.

I keep it on a weekly rhythm rather than a dedicated block — one problem set covering linear algebra, calculus, or probability every week, so it doesn't get crowded out by the bigger core-CS subjects.

Topics I'm covering

  • Linear Algebra — Matrices & Eigenvalues
  • Calculus — Limits, Continuity, Integrals
  • Probability & Statistics
  • Discrete Math Overlap Topics

PYQ notes

Probability questions are the ones I get wrong most often here — usually from misreading the question, not from not knowing the method.
