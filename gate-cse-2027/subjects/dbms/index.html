First pass, in progress

DBMS ends up being one of the more comforting subjects, because a lot of it is logical once the rules are clear — especially normalization, which used to feel like a memorization exercise until I understood why each normal form actually exists.

Working through relational algebra and transactions right now. SQL query-output questions are next, since those show up reliably in PYQs and reward careful reading more than deep theory.
