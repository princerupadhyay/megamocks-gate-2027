TOC

Theory of Computation

First pass

TOC felt intimidating from the name alone, but automata theory turned out to be more pattern recognition than abstract theory once I actually sat down with it. Turing machines and decidability are a different story.

Working through finite automata and regular languages now. Pushdown automata and Turing machines are next, and I'm expecting them to take longer than everything before it combined.

Topics I'm covering

  • Finite Automata — DFA & NFA
  • Regular Expressions & Languages
  • Context-Free Grammars & Pushdown Automata
  • Turing Machines
  • Decidability & Undecidability
  • Pumping Lemma

PYQ notes

Automata-construction questions are manageable so far. Decidability questions are the ones I'm avoiding a little, which usually means I need to face them sooner.
